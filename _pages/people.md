## Research Areas

Our lab focuses on several interconnected research areas:

- **AI-Generated Content Security**: Privacy preservation, controllability, authenticity, and compliance
- **Speech Privacy and Security**: Voice-enabled systems security and acoustic-channel attacks/defenses
- **Multimodal Data Privacy**: Cross-modal vulnerabilities and privacy-preserving technologies
- **IoT Security**: Smart device security frameworks and implementations
- **Adversarial Machine Learning**: Attack and defense mechanisms for AI systems
